## What it was

An AI education and deployment company focused on making AI practical and
accessible. I co-founded it in April 2023 and handed it off in January 2026 to
my co-founder, who is also my brother.

## What I did

- Built and ran a bootcamp program in partnership with HubSpot Academy that put
  over a thousand learners through hands-on, eight-week applied AI projects
  with real companies. I owned curriculum, facilitation, iteration, and the
  partner relationships behind it.
- Earned and maintained approved training vendor status under the Massachusetts
  Workforce Training Fund Express Program (Commonwealth Corporation),
  delivering state-funded AI training to eligible Massachusetts employers.
- Advised businesses on building internal AI Centers of Excellence.
- Grew the company from zero revenue to multiple six figures.
