## The problem

A live HubSpot Academy bootcamp Slack channel was drowning in repeated
questions. Instructors spent their time answering the same things instead of
teaching.

## What I built

An LLM support agent, GPT Trainer plus a custom Slack integration, deployed
directly into the cohort's working channel where students already were.

## Results

About 1,900 queries across 969 conversations over a nine-month cohort. I ran a
human-in-the-loop evaluation system to iterate on response quality throughout.

## What I learned

Shipping into a live cohort means every failure is visible immediately. That is
uncomfortable and it is also the fastest feedback loop I have ever had.
