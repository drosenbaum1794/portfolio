## What it was

Built and ran in partnership with HubSpot Academy. Students worked on real
problems for real HubSpot customers, not exercises. Over a thousand learners
came through it.

## What I owned

Curriculum design, facilitation, iteration from cohort feedback, and the
institutional partnerships that sourced learners, projects, and delivery
channels.

## What I learned

Watching a student go from unsure to capable over eight weeks is the most
satisfying work I have done.
